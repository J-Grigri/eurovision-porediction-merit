Eurovision Song Contest · 2004–2024 · hold-out 2025–2026 · with Eurostat migration and CEPII geography

Who votes for whom, and why it matters for prediction

Built only from the unofficial Eurovision dataset: 2,324 voter→country pairs from 20 contests. Every vote is compared with what all other countries gave the same song in the same show, so song quality cancels out and what remains is country bias. The 2025 and 2026 contests were never used for fitting and serve only as a final test.

Cultural: diaspora signal

Where the public and the jury disagree most

Same voting country, same songs. The pairs where the televote is far warmer than the jury mostly involve large emigrant communities: Romanians in Italy, Albanians in Switzerland and Italy, Lithuanians in Ireland and the UK. That fits diaspora voting, but this dataset can't prove it (see the last section).

Jury and public picked the same winner in 1 of 8 finals from 2016 to 2024.

Structural · the host question

Do hosts really do worse?

Since 2004, no host has won. The penalty survives controls for last year's score (regression to the mean) and for skipping the semi-final. But the host is almost always last year's winner, so hosting and defending the title can't be told apart. And before 2004, hosts often won.
